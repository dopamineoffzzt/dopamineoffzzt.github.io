Privacy Policy (EN)

In short: dopamineOff runs entirely on your phone. The app has no internet access and collects no data off your device: it does not send, share or sell anything, and it contains no ads or analytics. Everything the app stores stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall it (except backup files you export yourself). The only personal data the developer may receive is what you send yourself, for example an e-mail (section 6).

1. Who is responsible for the app

The dopamineOff app (package pl.dopamineoff) is developed and published by Przemysław Śliwiński, who is the controller of the personal data he receives from you (section 6). He has no access to the data stored in the app on your device. In Google Play the app is published under the developer name dopamineOff Team. Privacy contact: dopamineoff.zzt@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

dopamineOff blocks the apps you choose. To unlock an app for a limited time, you complete a short task (for example maths, sudoku, chess, a walk, jumping jacks or reading). You can also set quiet hours and daily limits.

3. What data the app processes on your device and why

All data below is processed only on your device. The app never sends it to the developer or to anyone else, and it does not have the permission to use the internet. Data leaves your phone only if you move it yourself, for example by sending someone your export file (section 7).

4. Permissions

The app asks you for these permissions (the app explains each one before it takes you to Android settings or Android asks you):

  • Usage access: to see which app is on screen and the time spent in blocked apps.
  • Display over other apps: to show the block screen over a blocked app.
  • Notifications: the unlock timer and the warning before an app is blocked again.
  • Physical activity (only if you choose the "Walk" task): to count steps.

In addition, the app declares technical permissions that Android grants without asking: running a foreground service with a persistent notification (to keep the block running and show the unlock timer) and starting after the phone restarts (so protection continues). It has no permission to use the internet. The app does not use accessibility services, device administration, exact alarms, location, contacts, the camera or the microphone. Google Play shows the full list of permissions on the app's page. You can revoke any permission at any time in Android settings. Without usage access and display over other apps, blocking does not work, but the rest of the app still does.

5. Sharing

The app does not share or sell any data. It contains no ads, analytics, crash reporting or third-party libraries that collect data, and it does not use Google Play in-app review, in-app updates or in-app purchases. The app is free.

Google Play may collect its own data about installs and crashes under the Google Privacy Policy; in Google Play Console the developer sees statistics and crash reports (for example the type of error, phone model and Android version). The app is built so that its own error messages do not include your data, such as the names of blocked apps or of your files.

6. When you contact the developer

If you write to dopamineoff.zzt@gmail.com, the developer receives your e-mail address and the content of your message (and anything you attach). They are used only to answer you and to handle your request, on the basis of the developer's legitimate interest in replying to messages (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Messages are kept for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, unless a longer period is needed to handle your request, and then deleted. The mailbox is a Gmail account, so the messages are also stored by Google, which handles them under the Google Privacy Policy and may process them outside the European Economic Area, for example in the USA; Google LLC participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The developer may also keep or disclose these messages when the law requires it, for example at the request of a public authority (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), or when they are needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Writing to the developer is voluntary; without it you can use the whole app. No decisions about you are made automatically and no profiling takes place.

If you post a review of the app in Google Play, it is published by Google under your Google Play profile. The developer can read it and reply publicly in Google Play, but does not store it anywhere else.

7. Backup (export and import)

In Settings you can export your settings and statistics to a JSON file in a place you choose (for example phone storage or your own cloud drive). The file contains, among other things, the names of blocked apps and your task history, which may reveal which apps you wanted to limit. The file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe; you decide where you keep it and who you give it to. The app never sends the export file anywhere.

The app is configured to stay out of Google's cloud backup. On Android 12 and later, Android's own phone-to-phone transfer (by cable or directly between phones), which you start yourself, can copy the app's data to a new phone; on older Android versions the app is excluded from it as well. Backup and transfer tools made by phone manufacturers may work differently.

8. Deleting data

You can delete the data the app stores by uninstalling it or in Android settings: Apps → dopamineOff → Storage → Clear data (the names may differ depending on the phone). This does not delete backup files you exported yourself (section 7), because they are stored outside the app, in a place you chose. You delete those files yourself, for example in a file manager or in your own cloud drive. The app has no user accounts, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. To have an e-mail conversation with the developer deleted, write to the address above.

9. Security

Data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access on a phone that is not rooted. The app sends nothing, so data from the app does not end up on any server. The export file (section 7) is not encrypted.

10. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children; its target audience declared in Google Play is people aged 18 and over. The app's age rating in Google Play, assigned through the IARC questionnaire, only means that the app contains no content unsuitable for younger users; it does not mean that the app is aimed at children. The app does not knowingly process children's data.

11. Your rights

You have full control over the data on your device: you can view it in the app, export it and delete it. For data the developer receives from you (section 6) you have the rights under the GDPR: access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and objection. Write to dopamineoff.zzt@gmail.com. The developer will answer within one month; in complex cases this period may be extended by up to two more months, and you will be told so within the first month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

12. Changes to this policy and language versions

If the app starts processing data differently, the developer will update this page and the date at the top before releasing that version of the app. Earlier versions of this page stay available in the change history of the public repository that hosts it. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This policy is published in English and Polish. Both versions have the same content and are equally valid; the developer keeps them identical.
